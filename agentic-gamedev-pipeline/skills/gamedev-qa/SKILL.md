# GameDev Runtime QA

Apply [assignment ownership and event routing](../gamedev-pipeline/references/stage-handoff-invariant.md#one-owner-and-one-current-assignment), then the [worker read route](../gamedev-pipeline/references/delivery-contract.md#worker-read-route) and [QA artifact contract](references/qa-output-contract.md). Load only named sections for encountered events. A supplied `control_binding` uses [Worker return](../gamedev-pipeline/references/control-return.md#worker-return); bounded caller work does not restart a stage. Standalone calls keep their public result.

Remain independent from Engineering and Review and read-only to persistent candidate files. Temporary authorized gameplay/input/console actions are permitted under [Environment actions](../gamedev-pipeline/references/interaction-evidence.md#environment-actions). Assess each source-bound assertion through its approved method and actual supported integration. Before serialization, establish applicability, exact case/proof locator, current environment and actual observations under [Independent assessment before serialization](../gamedev-pipeline/references/qa-acceptance-contract.md#independent-assessment-before-serialization).

Classify capability availability for every approved alternative, not from an identity prefix or a denied preferred method. Preserve actual security denials; a source-only method with no missing capability remains eligible. Continue independent safe scenarios after a defect; stop dependent ones and retain their truthful `not_run` result. Never infer pass from AUTO, a capability list, a green aggregate, a template claim or future controller execution. A proved false conditional prerequisite may yield `not_applicable`; do not claim its scenario ran.

Use controller-bound `context.machine_checks` and pending IDs. Missing applicable machine evidence follows [active checks](../gamedev-pipeline/references/execution-contract.md#check-inside-active-engineering-or-qa) under the exact grant or Director relay; never execute planned argv directly. Pause owned environment activity and consume the complete bound result/refreshed required input before continuing in the same host turn. A receipt supports only what its real cases and permitted proof establish; manual/device execution remains separate.

Investigate obstacles through available permitted routes and [technical assessment](../gamedev-pipeline/references/technical-decisions.md#resolve-before-escalating). Preserve prior applicable proof on rework. The journal records actual resolution without acceptance credit. Return fail for established product/test failures or concrete `verification_incomplete` acceptance gaps, preserving unrelated blocked assertions; use blocked only for a remaining external prerequisite or undelegated authority. Do not repair the candidate, alter acceptance or start a stage.

Return every issued identity/assertion in the exact schema under the [terminal artifact boundary](../gamedev-pipeline/references/stage-handoff-invariant.md#terminal-artifact-boundary). The exact terminal output is authorized even though product access is read-only.
